Hello! I'm Frouk, a passionate game modder and Software Engineer from Ukraine.<br>
My journey began in 2019 when I started modding offline games using Cheat Engine. Over time, I expanded my skills to include Unity Engine modding also using Cheat Engine with mono features.<br>
In my 9th grade, I took my time to research and learn C/C++ as well as Lua, x86/x64 Assembly and reverse engineering techinques.<br>
Currently I'm focused on this website, various game mods and researching further into game and software development.<br>
<br>
First game that I have modded was "Grand Theft Auto: San Andreas" back in 2019. It sparked my interest in game modding and development. Why GTA San Andreas? Since I didn't had powerful PC to handle games that were released that year<br>
I decided to start with something that my PC could handle. Over time I moved to more complex games and engines.<br>
Had an upgrade in 2023 which allowed me to explore more modern games and engines like Unity.<br>
