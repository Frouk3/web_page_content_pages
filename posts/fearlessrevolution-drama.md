## Introduction

Hey everyone,

If you have been keeping up with the recent discussions on Reddit and across the modding community, you probably already know that FearlessRevolution has completely lost its way. What used to be a great hub for the community has turned into a cash grab fueled by aggressive monetization, paywalls, and forced app installations. Since I have decided to step away from the site for good, I wanted to take a moment to explain what is actually going on behind the scenes and why I can no longer be a part of it.

## Why I Am Quitting

Starting today, I am officially cutting ties with FearlessRevolution and will no longer be uploading my cheat tables there. The site was originally built on a simple, great premise: hosting open-source cheat tables without paywalls or artificial barriers. Sadly, the people running the show have taken things in the exact opposite direction.

The biggest issue is how hard they are pushing their own ecosystem. They have developed a proprietary app—currently in beta—that seems specifically designed to lock down website access and funnel everyone into their software. Alongside that, they are heavily promoting ModEngine, which is basically a knock-off of Cheat Engine with a built-in table search. Just to use basic features, you are forced to download their app, and anything beyond the bare minimum gets locked behind premium fees. 

To make matters worse, the way they promote this is completely dishonest. A lot of the tables uploaded by ModEngine or their affiliated accounts on sites like Nexus Mods barely have a single working feature—sometimes they don't have any at all. They are purely bait meant to redirect you back to their website or trick you into downloading their app. If you look at the comments on those posts, they are usually flooded with fake accounts praising the "table" just to make it look legitimate to unsuspecting users.

If you try to call any of this out on their forums, you quickly realize how strict the censorship has become. Admins and moderators routinely scrub any negative feedback from the site. They have even set up an automated filter that censors the names of competing mod-hosting sites and replaces them with the words "fearlessrevolution." Even worse, users have caught the admins deliberately editing posts that criticize their app, swapping out their app's name for "Cheat Engine." It is a dirty tactic that shifts the blame onto innocent software and unfairly drags down the reputation of Cheat Engine and its creator, Dark Byte.

The final straw for me happened when I actually tried to leave. I went into my settings to delete my account, only to realize there is no option to do so. After I opened a thread asking the staff to remove my profile, the admins didn't delete my data—instead, they hijacked the account, changed my password, and renamed my profile to a random placeholder, "Musky22." 

Because of all this, I am moving everything over to my Discord server and my Nexus Mods page, where I will be hosting all of my cheat tables going forward.

## Conclusion

At the end of the day, FearlessRevolution has been completely ruined by greed. While some corners of the gaming community might be okay with paywalling certain types of mods, locking open-source cheat tables behind paywalls and shady software crosses a line I am not willing to support. 

Thank you so much for sticking with me and supporting my work. I look forward to seeing you all over on platforms that actually treat their creators and users with a bit of respect.
